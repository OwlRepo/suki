- Open Pipeline to monitor sales or service opportunities by stage.
- Move records to the correct stage after each customer interaction.
- Add follow-up tasks with realistic due dates.
- Keep stage notes short and specific so the next staff can continue work.
- Use Customers page when you need to update customer profile details.
- Common mistake: leaving deals in old stages after customer responds.
- Recovery: update stage immediately after each call or message.
- Common mistake: no due date on follow-up tasks.
- Recovery: always set due date and owner before closing the task modal.
