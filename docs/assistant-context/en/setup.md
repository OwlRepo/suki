- Open Setup during initial onboarding or after major business changes.
- Complete required business profile fields and operational preferences.
- Confirm appointment defaults and communication settings.
- Validate settings before staff begins daily usage.
- Use Onboarding route for guided replay if setup steps are unclear.
- Common mistake: partial setup then starting operations.
- Recovery: finish required setup checklist before handling live customers.
- Common mistake: skipping verification of contact channels.
- Recovery: test channels before launch day.
