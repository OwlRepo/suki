- Business name and type are the required setup details. No sample customer, recorded visit, or appointment is required.
- After saving business details, open Today. First appointment and sharing are optional actions, not access requirements.
- Existing businesses with unfinished legacy setup can use the workspace without repeating exercises.
- Completed setup and historical first-value timestamps are retained. Finishing business setup does not record a visit.
- If business creation fails, correct the error and retry without re-entering the form. If progress saving fails, use Retry; do not create the business twice.
- Edit business details later in Settings. Onboarding Demo is a separate demonstration, not a required production setup flow.
