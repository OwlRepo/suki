- Open Share Slots to generate booking links and QR codes.
- Confirm slot availability in Appointments before sharing.
- Copy the link and send through your preferred channel.
- Use QR for in-store display and link for chat/text campaigns.
- Test the shared link yourself before publishing broadly.
- Common mistake: old link shared after schedule change.
- Recovery: regenerate or recopy latest link from Share Slots.
- Common mistake: sharing without checking active business context.
- Recovery: verify active workspace before copying link.
