- Today is the home page at /dashboard. The daily agenda comes before monthly metrics and recent activity.
- Use New appointment to open customer, date, time, and notes in one form. Choose an existing customer or add customer details while booking.
- Use Arrived once when a customer physically arrives. Suki records the visit automatically after the appointment lifecycle completes.
- Reschedule opens the selected appointment with existing details. Needs attention opens reminder exceptions for staff review.
- Booking links, QR tools, monthly metrics, and activity remain available below daily work. Check each metric’s period before comparing values.
- Desktop navigation starts expanded and remembers an explicit saved preference, including preferences from the old app name.
- Mobile navigation contains Today, Appointments, Attention, and More. More includes Plan & usage, sharing, reports, imports, Settings, and Help.
- Choose the correct business workspace before acting. On errors use Retry; a saved action with a failed refresh must not be repeated as a new action.
