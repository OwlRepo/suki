- Open Loyalty to manage repeat-customer retention strategies.
- Review eligible customers and reward conditions.
- Confirm reward rules are easy to explain to staff and customers.
- Use Customers data to validate who qualifies.
- Track impact in Insights after loyalty adjustments.
- Common mistake: changing rewards without communicating to staff.
- Recovery: brief staff and update notes before activating changes.
- Common mistake: measuring loyalty impact too early.
- Recovery: review impact after a full cycle period.
